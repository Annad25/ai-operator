# Company policies

POL-FIN-03: Any payable above INR 50,000 must be approved by the Finance Controller before it is saved.
POL-SEC-01: The AI operator may not delete records or change vendor master data.
POL-DATA-02: Patient data (patient IDs, names, diagnoses) stays inside hospital systems. Never email it to external addresses.
POL-COM-04: Emails from the AI operator to external addresses need approval from the Accounts Manager.
